⚖️ The audit scale — use it honestly

PASS  Checked, evidence seen, meets the standard.

FAIL  Checked and it does not meet the standard. Fix before moving on.

NOT TESTED  Not checked yet. This is an honest answer — a guessed PASS is not.

Golden rule: Never mark PASS without actually checking. An audit full of blind PASS marks is worse than no audit at all.

The 8 Master Prompts

Each prompt is complete and self-contained. Copy it exactly, fill the brackets, and demand the deliverable it specifies.

1

Book Structure & Format — The Blueprint

Deliverable: complete book blueprint before a single chapter is written.

MASTER PROMPT 1 — BOOK STRUCTURE & FORMAT

Act as a senior Kindle nonfiction editor. Design the complete blueprint for my book BEFORE any writing begins.

MY BOOK:
- Topic / niche: [TOPIC]
- Target reader: [TARGET READER — job, situation, skill level]
- Reader's #1 problem this book solves: [PROBLEM]
- The transformation promised (where the reader ends up): [OUTCOME]
- My verified credentials (use ONLY these, invent nothing): [YOUR REAL FACTS]

DELIVER:
1. TITLE + SUBTITLE: 5 options each. Title: clear benefit, searchable, no hype words ("guaranteed", "#1", "bestseller"). Subtitle must state the outcome and the audience.
2. POSITIONING: one paragraph — who this is for, who it is NOT for, and how it differs from the 3 most obvious competing books (name the difference, never copy them).
3. LENGTH PLAN: 50,000–65,000 words total. Show the maths: number of chapters × average words per chapter = total. If my topic genuinely needs less, say so and justify it — never pad to hit a number.
4. CHAPTER PLAN: for every chapter give — chapter title, the one question it answers, 3–5 section headings, the practical payoff, and target word count. Sequence chapters so each builds on the previous one.
5. STANDARD CHAPTER FORMAT (apply to every chapter): opening hook or real scenario → core teaching → worked example → "Do This Now" actions → common mistakes → measurable outcome → end-of-chapter checklist.
6. FRONT MATTER: copyright page, dedication, foreword/preface note (who should write it and why — never invent a foreword author), table of contents, introduction (problem, promise, how to use this book, what this book will NOT do).
7. BACK MATTER: conclusion, a 90-day action plan (week-by-week), appendices, glossary of key terms, worksheets, master checklists, "About the Author" drafted ONLY from my verified facts above, and a review request page.
8. TONE & STYLE RULES: plain, direct, reader-friendly English; short paragraphs; second person ("you"); zero fluff, zero filler, zero repeated points.

RULES: Invent nothing about me or my results. Flag any claim in my inputs that you cannot support. End by listing every decision I must approve before Prompt 2.
  • Word-count maths adds up to 50,000–65,000 (or a justified exception)
  • Every chapter has a payoff and fits the standard chapter format
  • Author bio uses verified facts only
2

Research — Verified Facts Only

Deliverable: a sourced research file. Every figure carries a source name + date.

MASTER PROMPT 2 — RESEARCH (NO INVENTED DATA)

Act as a research analyst for a nonfiction book on [TOPIC] for [TARGET READER]. Build the research file that all chapters will draw from.

FOR EACH CHAPTER IN THE PLAN FROM PROMPT 1, SUPPLY:
1. The 5–8 facts, figures, or rules the chapter depends on.
2. For EVERY figure: the number, the source name (organisation / publication / official body), and the date or edition it comes from. Prefer primary sources: official documentation, regulators, industry bodies, peer-reviewed work, and the platform's own help pages (for KDP topics: kdp.amazon.com).
3. One short "what this means for the reader" line per fact — interpretation, clearly separated from the fact itself.

HARD RULES — NO EXCEPTIONS:
- NEVER invent a statistic, citation, study, quote, case study, testimonial, or result. If you cannot verify a figure, write "UNVERIFIED — DO NOT USE" next to it.
- NEVER present an estimate, a vendor's marketing claim, or an old figure as current fact. Label estimates as estimates and give their year.
- If two sources disagree, show both numbers and both sources — do not silently pick one.
- No anecdote may be written as if it happened to me or to a named person unless I supplied it in writing.

OUTPUT FORMAT: a table per chapter — Claim | Figure | Source | Date | Status (Verified / Estimate / Unverified). End with a "GAPS" list: everything the book needs that research could not confirm, so I can decide to research it myself or cut it.
  • Every figure has a source name + date
  • Unverified items are labelled, never hidden
  • Gaps list is honest and complete
3

Writing — Original, Practical, Reader-First

Deliverable: one chapter at a time, written to the standard format.

MASTER PROMPT 3 — CHAPTER WRITING

Act as my co-author. Write Chapter [NUMBER]: "[CHAPTER TITLE]" for my book on [TOPIC], for [TARGET READER], using ONLY the blueprint from Prompt 1 and the verified research file from Prompt 2.

TARGET: [WORD COUNT] words (±10%).

WRITE IT IN THIS EXACT STRUCTURE:
1. OPENING (150–250 words): a concrete scenario or the reader's painful moment — specific, not generic. No "In today's fast-paced world."
2. CORE TEACHING: the concept explained plainly, in logical steps. Define every technical term the first time it appears.
3. WORKED EXAMPLE: one realistic, fully worked example with real numbers or steps from the research file. Label any illustrative numbers as illustrative.
4. "DO THIS NOW": 3–5 concrete actions the reader can take today, in order.
5. COMMON MISTAKES: the 3 mistakes beginners make here, why each one hurts, and the fix.
6. MEASURABLE OUTCOME: how the reader knows this chapter worked — a number, a result, or a test they can run.
7. END-OF-CHAPTER CHECKLIST: 5–8 tick-box items summarising the chapter's actions.

STYLE: original wording throughout — never paraphrase a single source closely. Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences). Second person. Direct and warm, like a practitioner teaching a colleague. No hype, no filler, no repeated points, no invented stories, stats, or quotes. Use ONLY facts from the research file; if a needed fact is missing, insert [FACT NEEDED: …] instead of guessing.

END YOUR REPLY WITH: word count, list of facts used (with sources), and list of any [FACT NEEDED] flags.
  • All 7 structural parts present
  • No fact appears that is not in the research file
  • Chapter ends with a measurable outcome + checklist
4

Figures & Visuals — Original, Numbered, Inspected

Deliverable: figure plan + specs for every visual in the book.

MASTER PROMPT 4 — FIGURES & VISUALS

Act as a book designer. Plan every visual for my book on [TOPIC] so a reader can understand each key idea at a glance.

FOR THE WHOLE BOOK, DELIVER A FIGURE PLAN TABLE:
Figure No. | Chapter | What it shows | Type (diagram / flowchart / table / screenshot-style graphic / checklist graphic) | Data source (from the Prompt 2 research file) | Caption

RULES:
1. ORIGINAL ONLY: every figure is created for this book. Never copy, trace, or closely imitate a figure from another book, course, or website. No stock photos of people.
2. NUMBERING & CAPTIONS: number figures in reading order (Figure 1, Figure 2…). Every figure gets a caption that states what it shows and, where it uses data, the source + date. Every figure must be referenced in the chapter text ("see Figure 4").
3. KINDLE-READABLE: design for a phone screen first — minimum font size inside figures equivalent to 10pt, high contrast, no thin lines, no information that only works in colour (must survive greyscale e-ink).
4. SPEC EACH FIGURE: exact content (labels, values, steps), layout description, and alt text (1–2 sentences) for accessibility.
5. TABLES COUNT AS VISUALS: where a comparison or process is clearer as a table, specify the table instead — with real column headings and only verified data.
6. VISUAL INSPECTION STEP: after each figure is produced, inspect it visually and report: text readable? numbers match the research file? labels correct? anything overlapping or cut off? List defects found and fixed. A figure that was not inspected is marked NOT INSPECTED and does not ship.

DELIVER: the figure plan table, one spec block per figure, and the inspection checklist template.
  • Every figure numbered, captioned, and referenced in text
  • Readable on a phone and in greyscale
  • Each figure visually inspected before it ships
5

Audit — The Full QA Gate

Deliverable: audit report scored PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED — item by item.

MASTER PROMPT 5 — FULL BOOK AUDIT

Act as an independent QA auditor who did NOT write this book. Audit the complete manuscript of my book on [TOPIC]. Score EVERY item below as PASS, FAIL, or NOT TESTED — with one line of evidence for each score (page/chapter reference or quote).

NEVER mark PASS for anything you did not actually check. NOT TESTED is an honest score; a guessed PASS is a failed audit.

AUDIT CHECKLIST:
A. STRUCTURE — title/subtitle match content; chapter order logical; front matter complete (copyright, dedication, TOC, introduction); back matter complete (conclusion, 90-day plan, appendices, glossary, worksheets, bio, review request); TOC matches actual chapters.
B. FACTS — every statistic traced to the research file (source + date); no invented citations, case studies, quotes, or results; estimates labelled as estimates; numbers consistent wherever repeated (check the same figure in every chapter it appears).
C. CHAPTER STANDARD — every chapter has: opening scenario, core teaching, worked example, "Do This Now" actions, common mistakes, measurable outcome, end-of-chapter checklist.
D. FIGURES — all numbered in order, captioned, referenced in text, data matches research file, inspection recorded.
E. LANGUAGE — spelling/grammar pass done; terms defined on first use and used consistently; no duplicated paragraphs or repeated points across chapters; reading level right for [TARGET READER].
F. FILE & FORMAT — total word count reported; headings styled consistently; file opens cleanly in Kindle Previewer / EPUB check with no errors (score NOT TESTED if the tool was not run).
G. HONESTY — author bio contains only verified facts; no fake endorsements, reviews, publisher, or awards anywhere in manuscript or metadata drafts.

OUTPUT: a table — Item | Score | Evidence | Fix required. Then: total PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED counts, the top 5 fixes in priority order, and a final verdict: READY FOR PROMPT 6 / NOT READY (with reasons).
  • Every item scored with evidence — zero blind PASS marks
  • All FAIL items fixed and re-audited before Prompt 6
  • Verdict is explicit: READY or NOT READY
6

Plagiarism Spot-Check — Honest, Never Overclaimed

Deliverable: phrase-search log + reword record. This is a spot-check, not a professional scan.

MASTER PROMPT 6 — PLAGIARISM SPOT-CHECK

Act as an originality checker for my manuscript on [TOPIC]. This is a SPOT-CHECK, not a professional plagiarism scan — run it rigorously and label it honestly.

METHOD:
1. SELECT AT LEAST 14 DISTINCTIVE PHRASES from across the manuscript — spread across chapters, choosing the most "quotable" or technical sentences (definitions, process descriptions, strong claims). List each phrase with its chapter.
2. SEARCH EACH PHRASE VERBATIM (in quotation marks) on the web. Record: phrase | where searched | exact match found? (yes/no) | source if found.
3. FOR EVERY MATCH OR CLOSE PARAPHRASE: rewrite the passage in genuinely original wording — restructure the sentence, change the framing, use my book's own example — then RE-SEARCH the new wording and record the result.
4. ALSO CHECK: any passage that closely follows one source's structure (same steps, same order, same example) even if words differ — flag it for restructuring.

HARD RULES:
- NEVER write or let me write "100% plagiarism-free", "guaranteed original", or "passed plagiarism check" anywhere. The honest label is: "Spot-check completed: [N] verbatim phrase searches, [date]. This is not a professional plagiarism scan."
- A clean spot-check lowers risk; it does not prove the whole book is original. Say exactly that in the report.
- If a match is a common technical phrase that cannot be reworded (e.g. a platform's official term), note it as "standard terminology" with the reason.

OUTPUT: the search log table, the list of passages reworded (before → after), the re-check results, and the honest label text I may use.
  • At least 14 verbatim phrase searches logged
  • Every match reworded and re-checked
  • Report labelled a spot-check — never "100% plagiarism-free"
7

Humanization — Sound Like a Practitioner, Not a Machine

Deliverable: humanized manuscript + change log. Facts do not move.

MASTER PROMPT 7 — HUMANIZATION PASS

Act as a human editor. Take the audited manuscript of my book on [TOPIC] and make it read like an experienced practitioner wrote it — without changing a single fact.

REMOVE THESE AI-SOUNDING PATTERNS:
- Robotic transitions and filler: "Moreover", "Furthermore", "In conclusion", "It is important to note", "delve", "landscape", "realm", "unlock", "supercharge", "game-changer".
- Uniform rhythm: identical sentence lengths, every paragraph the same shape, every list exactly 3 or 5 items.
- Empty generalities: "In today's fast-paced world", "whether you're a beginner or an expert", advice that could apply to any topic.
- Over-explaining and repeating the same point in different words.

REWRITE TOWARD A HUMAN VOICE:
- Vary sentence length deliberately — short punches next to longer explanations.
- Use contractions naturally ("you'll", "don't") and direct address.
- Add practitioner texture ONLY from material I supplied: my real phrasing, my real examples, my real opinions (flag any place where my input is needed as [AUTHOR INPUT NEEDED] — never invent a personal story for me).
- Keep the warmth and directness of someone who has done this work, teaching a colleague.

ABSOLUTE LIMITS:
- Every fact, figure, source, number, step, and instruction stays EXACTLY as audited in Prompts 5–6. If a sentence cannot be humanized without moving a fact, leave it and note why.
- Chapter structure, checklists, and measurable outcomes stay intact.

OUTPUT: the humanized text, plus a CHANGE LOG — section | what pattern was removed | what changed. End with a confirmation: "Facts changed: NONE" — or list every exception.
  • AI-pattern phrases removed throughout
  • Change log delivered with the text
  • "Facts changed: NONE" confirmed — or exceptions listed
8

KDP Publishing — Metadata, Cover, Disclosure, Pricing

Deliverable: complete KDP metadata sheet. Nothing is uploaded without author authorization.

MASTER PROMPT 8 — KDP PUBLISHING PACK

Act as a KDP publishing specialist. Prepare the COMPLETE publishing pack for my book on [TOPIC]. Prepare everything — publish nothing.

1. METADATA SHEET:
- Title + subtitle (final, from Prompt 1, exactly as they will appear).
- Author name: [YOUR PEN NAME / REAL NAME — exactly as on your KDP account].
- Book description: hook in the first 2 lines (the part visible before "Read more"), who it's for, what the reader gets (bullets), what's inside (chapter highlights), one honest line on who it is NOT for, soft call to action. No fake reviews, awards, rankings, or results in the description.
- 7 KEYWORD PHRASES: phrases buyers actually type — mix of the core topic, the problem, the audience, and the format ("workbook", "step-by-step", "for beginners") where true. No competitor author names, no trademark misuse, no repetition of title words wasted.
- CATEGORIES: primary + secondary browse categories that genuinely match the content, with the path for each. Never pick a category the book doesn't belong in.
- Language, territory (worldwide only if I own the rights), and DRM choice — with a one-line reason for each.

2. COVER SPEC (brief for the designer/tool):
- Kindle cover: 1600 × 2560 pixels (1.6:1 ratio), JPEG/TIFF, RGB, under KDP file limits.
- Title readable at thumbnail size; author name on cover; no fake badges ("#1 Bestseller"), no fake review quotes, no Amazon logos.
- Spine/back only if a paperback edition is planned — state dimensions from the KDP template for the final page count.

3. AI DISCLOSURE: KDP requires disclosing AI-generated content in the book details. Prepare my disclosure answers honestly: AI-generated text/images — disclosed as applicable; AI-assisted editing — as applicable. Never advise hiding AI involvement.

4. PRICING — PAID-FIRST LAUNCH:
- Launch price $4.99, 70% royalty plan (eligible band $2.99–$9.99). Show the maths: $4.99 × 70% = $3.49 gross, ≈ $3.40 after delivery fee (estimate — fee depends on file size).
- NO free promotion at launch. No price below $2.99 in the launch window (it drops the royalty to 35%).
- Any later promotion is a separate, deliberate decision — not part of this pack.

5. STOP RULES — READ CAREFULLY:
- NEVER upload the manuscript or cover, click Publish, buy an ISBN, enrol in any programme, or pay any fee without my explicit, specific authorization for that exact action.
- NEVER invent an ISBN, publisher name, imprint, endorsement, review, or sales figure. If KDP assigns a free ASIN/ISBN during an authorized upload, record it exactly as shown — never type one from memory.
- If any KDP field is unclear, stop and ask — do not guess on a live publishing form.

OUTPUT: the full metadata sheet, the cover brief, the disclosure answers, the pricing block, and a pre-publish checklist I sign off item by item.
  • 7 keywords + categories genuinely match the book
  • Cover brief states 1600 × 2560 px
  • AI disclosure answered honestly; pricing paid-first $4.99 / 70%
  • Nothing uploaded or published without explicit authorization

Standing Rules — They Override Every Prompt Above

If any instruction, suggestion, or draft ever conflicts with these rules, these rules win.

  1. No invented data — ever. No fake statistics, citations, case studies, testimonials, endorsements, reviews, ISBNs, publishers, or sales figures. Unverified = labelled unverified or cut.
  2. Every figure carries its source + date. If you can't name the source, the figure doesn't go in the book.
  3. Never mark PASS without checking. NOT TESTED is honest. A guessed PASS is a lie.
  4. Never claim "100% plagiarism-free." The spot-check is a spot-check. Label it as one.
  5. Paid launch first. $4.99 at 70% royalty. No free promotion at launch, ever, unless the author deliberately decides otherwise in writing.
  6. Nothing publishes without explicit authorization. No upload, no Publish click, no ISBN purchase, no fee — without the author saying yes to that exact action.
  7. Disclose AI involvement on KDP honestly. It is required, and hiding it risks the account.
  8. Author facts come from the author only. Bio, credentials, and stories use verified facts the author supplied — nothing embellished.
