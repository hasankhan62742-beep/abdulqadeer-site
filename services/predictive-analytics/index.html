Decisions that stop being guesswork

01Customers leave without warning

Churn is usually visible in the data weeks before it happens. A churn forecast scores every customer by risk, so your team can act while there is still time to keep them.

02Inventory planned by gut feel

Too much stock ties up cash; too little loses sales. Demand forecasting turns your sales history into per-product forecasts your purchasing can plan around.

03Risk judged inconsistently

Credit, order and case decisions vary by person and by day. A risk score ranks every case the same way, so human review goes where it matters most.

04Years of data, zero predictions

Your CRM and sales history already contain the patterns. The work is cleaning that data, building the right forecast, and proving it beats the simple approach.
