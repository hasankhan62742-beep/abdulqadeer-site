Decisions that stop being guesswork

01Customers leave without warning

Churn is usually visible in the data weeks before it happens. A churn model scores every customer by risk, so your team can act while there is still time to keep them.

02Inventory planned by gut feel

Too much stock ties up cash; too little loses sales. Demand forecasting turns your sales history into per-product forecasts your purchasing can plan around.

03Risk judged inconsistently

Credit, order and case decisions vary by person and by day. A risk score ranks every case the same way, so human review goes where it matters most.

04Years of data, zero predictions

Your CRM and sales history already contain the patterns. The work is cleaning that data, training the right model, and proving it beats the simple baseline.

From raw data to working predictions

1

Data audit

We look at what you actually have: volume, quality, history. If the data cannot support a reliable model, I will tell you before you spend anything.

2

Baseline first

A simple baseline sets the bar. The model only ships if it clearly beats doing things the obvious way.

3

Build & validate

Training on historical data, testing on held-out data the model never saw. Accuracy, errors and blind spots documented.

4

Deploy & hand over

API or dashboard live, documentation delivered, and a clear path for retraining as new data arrives.

Straight answers
