Where AI projects usually stall

01Demos that never reach production

Notebook experiments impress in a meeting and die before a real user touches them. Production ML is a different discipline: data pipelines, validation, deployment, monitoring.

02Predictions nobody trusts

A model without validation or explanations is a black box your team quietly ignores. Trust is earned with evidence — held-out scores and reasons for every prediction.

03Manual visual and document work

People inspecting images or reading documents one by one, at human speed and human error rates. Vision and NLP systems do the first pass; people handle the judgement calls.

04Generic tools that do not fit

Off-the-shelf AI does not know your products, your customers or your documents. Custom models are trained on exactly the data your decisions depend on.
