PRIVACY POLICY

Your information, explained plainly.

This page describes what this website actually collects, why, and where it goes — based on the technology the site really uses. Last updated: 22 October 2026.

What is collected

Very little, and only when you choose to share it. This is a static website hosted on Cloudflare Pages — there are no user accounts, no logins for visitors, and no advertising trackers run by this site.

01Inquiry form details

If you use the project inquiry form, the details you type — your name, email, business type, project goal, budget range, timeline and project description — are used to respond to your inquiry. Nothing more.

02Messages you send

If you email or message on WhatsApp, the contents of that conversation and your contact details are kept so the conversation can continue and any agreed work can be delivered.

03Basic visit data

Like most websites, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical logs — such as IP address, browser type and pages requested — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

04Analytics (if enabled)

Google Analytics may be used to understand visits in aggregate — which pages are read, roughly where visitors come from. IP addresses are anonymized, and the data is used to improve the site, not to identify you personally.

How the inquiry form works

The form on this site does not silently send anything. When you press send, it opens WhatsApp or your own email app with your answers pre-filled as a message — the message only leaves your device when you press send inside your own app.

When enabled, a copy of the inquiry is also stored in a private CRM database (hosted on Supabase, a cloud database provider) so your request can be tracked and followed up. Access to that database is restricted to the site owner. Your details are used only to respond to your inquiry and, if we agree to work together, to deliver that work. Your personal data is never sold.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own for visitors. The hosting platform may use essential cookies required for security and delivery. If Google Analytics is enabled, it may set analytics cookies to count visits; these are used in aggregate and are not used for advertising by this site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time — the site will still work.

Third-party services

▸Cloudflare

Hosts and delivers this website, and processes basic technical logs for security and performance.

▸Supabase

Hosts the private CRM database where inquiries are stored when that feature is enabled.

▸Calendly

If you book a call, scheduling happens on Calendly and is covered by Calendly's own privacy policy.

▸WhatsApp & your email provider

If you choose to make contact through them, your message travels through their platforms under their respective policies.

Retention & your choices

Inquiries are kept only as long as they are relevant — while we are in conversation or working together — and are deleted when they are no longer needed. You can ask at any time, by email, what information of yours is held, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted entirely. Requests are handled personally by the site owner and answered by email; no request needs a reason.
